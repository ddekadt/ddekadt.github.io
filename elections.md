## election analysis

Maps and analysis of South African election results.

**[South Africa's 2026 local government elections](/elections/sa-2026/)** (with Lethokuhle Sikosana). Ward-level maps of the November 2026 results and the swing since 2021: vote shares, council control, turnout and registration, an interactive ward map, and a tool for comparing the results with census data.
