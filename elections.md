## election analysis

During election season I tend to produce analyses and visualisations of the results. Here you can find some of that work.

<br>

**[South Africa's 2026 local government elections](/elections/sa-2026/)** (with Lethokuhle Sikosana). Ward-level maps of the November 2026 results and the swing since 2021: vote shares, council control, turnout and registration, an interactive ward map, and a tool for analysing what census characteristics are associated with those results.
